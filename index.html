The Qi Lab investigates the molecular mechanisms of neurodegenerative diseases using cryo-electron microscopy (cryo-EM) and in situ structural biology. Our research focuses on tau aggregation, MAPT mutations, and protein misfolding processes underlying dementia and Parkinsonism.
