Research

Tau & Neurodegeneration

We investigate how tau aggregation drives neurodegenerative diseases, combining structural biology with neuropathology.

Cryo-EM & Cryo-ET

We use state-of-the-art cryo-electron microscopy and tomography to resolve structures of disease-related assemblies at near-atomic resolution.

Protein Aggregation Mechanisms

Our work focuses on the formation, propagation, and toxicity of amyloid filaments in human brain tissue.
