Principal Investigator

Chao Qi （亓超）, Assistant Professor, Peking University.

Chao studied Biomedicine at Jilin University. He joined Da-Cheng Wang group as a Master student in the CAS Institute of Biophysics,China and was trained by macrocrystallography. He did a PhD in the Volodymyr Korkhov group at the ETH Zurich and Paul Scherre Institute, Switzerland. During his PhD, Chao focused on key membrane proteins within the signal transduction pathway, including i,Molecular architecture of Adenylyl Cyclase based signal transduction complex. ii,Structural basis of sterol recognition by human hedgehog receptor PTCH1.iii,Structures of connexin gap junction and hemi-channels. At 2022,he joined Sjors Schere and Michel Goedert group at MRC-LMB,UK as a postdoc to study tau filaments in neurodegenerative disease using cryo-EM helical reconstruction and in situ cryo-ET methods. Chao established his independent research group at the Department of Biophysics， School of Basic Medical Sciences, Peking University in 2025.

We are interest in molecular Neuroscience from physiology to pathology. Using biophysics,structural biology and computational methods, the lab aims to uncover the molecular mechanism of neurodegenerative diseases and translate structural insights into therapeutic strategies.
