Principal Investigator

Chao Qi, Ph.D.

Dr. Chao Qi is a structural biologist studying the molecular basis of neurodegenerative diseases. His research focuses on the structure, assembly, and propagation of amyloid filaments involved in Alzheimer’s disease, frontotemporal dementia, and Parkinson’s disease.

Using cryo-electron microscopy (cryo-EM) and cryo-electron tomography (cryo-ET), the lab aims to uncover disease-specific filament structures and translate these discoveries into therapeutic strategies targeting pathogenic protein aggregates.
